# Claims that must survive style changes
An association does not establish causation. A non-significant result does not establish equivalence or no effect. A sample-specific observation is not a population guarantee.
Keep percentage points distinct from relative percentage change. Keep confidence intervals, sample size, units and comparator together.
Keep uncertainty and conditional scope: may, observed, preliminary, under these conditions, and independent-validation requirements cannot be deleted merely for fluency.
Identifiers such as [S1] in a synthetic fixture refer to its provided source packet, not a fabricated publication.
Do not make a citation support a broader claim than the source supplies. If source material is missing, preserve the gap rather than filling it with a plausible statement.
